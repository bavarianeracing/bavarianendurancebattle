HELP BRING THE BEB PARADE OF HONOR TO LIFE.

TWELVE HOURS DESERVES MORE THAN A CHECKERED FLAG.

Building an endurance race car takes months of work, sacrifice and teamwork. Racing one for twelve hours asks even more.

When the race is over, we want the drivers, builders, mechanics and crews who took on that challenge to have a moment that belongs to them.

Bavarian Endurance Battle is developing the BEB Parade of Honor, a closing celebration where the teams and the people behind the cars become the stars.

A decorated parade truck would lead the procession with upbeat Bavarian music, blue and white flags and hosts in traditional Bavarian attire. Behind it, the illuminated BEB Safety Car would lead the top three overall finishing teams, proudly carrying their championship trophies.

Then comes the field. Race cars. Drivers. Mechanics. Crew members. The people who spent twelve hours keeping those BMWs alive.

Spectators would line the route with flags as the teams make their way toward the awards ceremony.

And finishing the race would not be a requirement for joining the celebration. Repaired cars could rejoin the field. If a BMW could not move under its own power, its crew could walk the parade route without it.

If you fought the Battle, you belong in the celebration.
