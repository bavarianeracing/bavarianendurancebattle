The BMW Car & Motorcycle Show

Every Generation.
Every Passion.

Whether preserved in original condition, carefully restored, or transformed into something extraordinary, every BMW has a story. Join fellow enthusiasts at Buttonwillow Raceway Park for a celebration of the machines and people behind the marque.

The show welcomes BMW automobiles and BMW motorcycles only. Both original and modified examples are encouraged. MINI vehicles are not eligible.

Automobile Awards

Classic BMW — Original & Restored

Preservation, restoration, historical character, and factory presentation.

Classic BMW — Modified

Older BMWs thoughtfully reimagined through design, engineering, and craftsmanship.

Modern BMW — Original & Stock

Factory configuration, condition, preparation, and attention to detail.

Modern BMW — Modified M

Modified genuine BMW M models, recognizing quality of execution and presentation.

Modern BMW — Modified Non-M

Modified non-M BMW models, celebrating creativity, craftsmanship, and execution.

Motorcycle Awards

BMW Motorcycle Heritage — Original & Restored

Historical character, preservation, restoration, and presentation.

BMW Motorcycle Modern — Original & Stock

Factory character, condition, preparation, and attention to detail.

BMW Motorcycle Custom & Modified

Design, fabrication, engineering, and quality of execution.

The model-year distinction between Classic/Heritage and Modern categories will be announced before registration opens.
