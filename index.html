Welcome to the Bavarian Endurance Battle

This is more than a race. It is an invitation to become part of BMW history.

Bavarian Endurance Battle brings generations of BMWs together in Southern California for a 12 hour endurance battle celebrating the machines, the people, and the passion that have carried the BMW legacy across generations.

Every BMW arrives with a story.

Some are prepared in professional race shops. Others are built after work in garages, backyards, and barns, one bolt, one repair, one late night at a time. But when the green flag falls, where they came from no longer matters.

History does not care where greatness begins. It remembers what happens when the moment arrives.

For twelve hours, driver, team, and machine will face more than their competitors. They will face heat, fatigue, mechanical failure, strategy, doubt, and time itself.

Every lap will ask the same question: Will you continue?

Because speed may win a lap.

Endurance earns respect.

And when the checkered flag finally falls, the racing ends, but what was created here remains. The impossible repairs, the friendships forged under pressure, the competitors who stopped to help one another, the machines that refused to surrender, and the teams that discovered they could endure more than they ever imagined.

That is why Bavarian Endurance Battle will celebrate more than podiums. We will honor perseverance, craftsmanship, sportsmanship, service, community, and those who embody the true spirit of endurance.

Because how you race can be remembered long after where you finished.

The race happens here, but the invitation reaches everywhere.

Teams and drivers from across California, across the country, and beyond are invited to bring their BMWs, their stories, and their determination to the grid.

Every generation has a legacy.
Every BMW has a story.
Every team has a battle ahead.

The next chapter is waiting.

Bring your BMW. Bring your team. Bring everything you have.

Why this exists

We want to race too.

There are BMWs built in famous race shops, and BMWs built in one-car garages after work. We respect both.

BEB is being built around a simple belief: your passion should matter more than the size of your budget. The backyard builder should be able to line up shoulder-to-shoulder with an established BMW shop and know that both earned their place by showing up prepared.

The Bavarian Endurance Battle was not created to celebrate the most expensive BMW. It was created to celebrate the people who refused to give up on building one.

My BMW Belongs Here
The BMW community

Race it. Show it. Build it. Support it.

BEB is bigger than the starting grid. The vision includes a BMW car show, festival atmosphere, vendors, families and a paddock where enthusiasts can actually connect.

You do not need a roll cage to belong here. If your BMW is part of your story, we want to know about it.

This community should look like the BMW community itself.

Women behind the wheel. Women building cars. First-time racers. Veteran competitors. Backyard builders. Professional shops. Families. Volunteers. Photographers. Clubs. Car-show participants.

If BMW is part of your story, there is a place for you here.
