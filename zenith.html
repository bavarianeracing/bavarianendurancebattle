Zukunft

The future doesn't wait for the corner.

BMW chose the letter Z from the German word Zukunft, meaning future. When the Z3 arrived, BMW's idea of the future was refreshingly simple: two seats, engine in front, power to the rear wheels and as little automobile as necessary surrounding the person driving it.

The Z3 took that philosophy racing, with the Roadster and especially the Coupé appearing in GT and endurance competition. The Germans even gave the Z3 M Coupé a nickname: Turnschuh, literally “gym shoe” or “sports shoe.” Small, unusual and remarkably light on its feet, the name fit perfectly.

Then BMW kept sharpening the idea.

The Z4 eventually evolved into the BMW Z4 GT3, a serious international endurance racing machine. It fought at the Nürburgring, Spa and circuits around the world, finishing second overall at the 2015 Nürburgring 24 Hours before achieving its greatest victory that same year: winning the 24 Hours of Spa outright.

The funny little two seat BMW had grown up.

And apparently it had learned how to fight.

Welcome To The Zenith Class

That's The Weapon.

Bavarian Endurance Battle created the Zenith Class for the smallest, lowest and perhaps most irritating members of the family.

While the bigger BEB categories can bring horsepower, torque and intimidating numbers, the Zenith Class brings considerably less car.
